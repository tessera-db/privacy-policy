Tessera for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 Contact: tesseradb@gmail.com

Tessera is a personal database app for Android. It works fully offline: your libraries, records, attachments and settings are stored on your device. We, the developer, operate no servers and never receive your data. This policy explains what the app stores, the one optional feature that sends data off your device, and how to delete everything.

01Summary

  • No account with us, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, no trackers.
  • Your data stays on your device unless you turn on Google Drive sync for a library.
  • Tessera is free. An optional one-time Tessera Pro purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.
  • With sync on, that library is copied only to your own Google Drive. We cannot see it.

02Data stored on your device

  • Your content: libraries, tables, records, attachments (photos and files you add), dashboards and scripts. Stored in the app's private storage.
  • Settings and sync status: preferences, the name of your Drive folder, and the time of the last sync.
  • Crash logs: if the app crashes, a short technical log is saved in the app's private storage to help diagnose the problem. It is never sent anywhere automatically.

Optional library locks use your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock. They hide a library from someone holding your unlocked phone; they do not encrypt the data. The app never receives your biometric data.

03Google Drive sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync for a library:

  • You sign in with your own Google account using Google's sign-in page (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE). The app on your device receives an access token and your Google account email address. The email is shown in the app so you know which account is connected. It is also stored with each record you create, so synced records show who added them.
  • The app requests permission to create files in your Google Drive and to access only the files it creates. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • The library's records, attachments and settings are uploaded to a Tessera folder in your Google Drive and kept up to date across your devices.
  • The data travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) directly between your device and Google. It is stored in your Drive in a readable form, under Google's terms and privacy policy, and anyone you give access to that folder can read it.
  • When you turn sync off for a library, Tessera asks whether to keep the library's copy in your Drive or move it to your Drive trash, where Google keeps it for 30 days. Your records on the device are not affected.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on your device, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore where the device supports it. They are sent only to Google.
  • Tessera's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide the sync feature, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people.

04Purchases

Tessera Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under Google's terms and privacy policy; Tessera never sees your card details, billing address or name. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Tessera Pro and stores a single "unlocked" flag on your device. No purchase information is sent to us. Receipts and refunds are handled by Google Play.

05Reminders and notifications

When you set a reminder on a date field, the reminder time is stored in the app's private database on your device. Android schedules the alarm and Tessera creates the notification on your device. Nothing about your reminders is sent anywhere.

06Home-screen widgets

If you add a Tessera widget, it shows the library name and the item you chose (and, for metric widgets, its current value) on your home screen, where anyone holding your unlocked phone can see it. Widgets for locked libraries show "Locked" instead of their contents. Widgets read from the app's database on your device and send nothing anywhere.

07Data we share

We do not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. When you use Drive sync, your device sends the library to Google Drive at your request. Backups and CSV exports go only where you choose in the system file picker. CSV files you import are read once through the system picker and copied into a new table.

08Permissions

Internet
used by Google Drive sync, for libraries you turn sync on for, and by Google Play for the Pro purchase.
Network state
used to skip sync attempts while you are offline.
Notifications
to show reminders you set. Asked for only when you set your first reminder.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)
to deliver a reminder at the minute you chose. You can refuse; reminders may then arrive a little later.
Run at startup
to re-arm your reminders after your phone restarts.
Biometrics
to unlock a library you have locked with your fingerprint or face. Android handles the check; Tessera never receives biometric data.
Google Play billing
to offer the optional Tessera Pro purchase.

Tessera requests no other permissions. Photos and files you attach are chosen through the system picker, which gives the app access to the selected items only.

09Retention and deletion

  • In the app: delete individual items, or use Settings → Delete all data to erase everything on the device.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device. Android's cloud backup is turned off for Tessera.
  • Google sign-in: Drive settings → Sign out removes the tokens from your device. You can also remove Tessera's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Drive copies: signing out does not delete files from your Drive, because they are your data. Delete the Tessera folder in Google Drive to remove them.

We keep no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

10Security

Data sent to Google Drive is protected in transit by HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are encrypted on the device. Scripts you write run in a sandbox with no network or file access. Data stored on your device and in your Drive is not encrypted by Tessera, so protect your phone with a screen lock and your Google account with 2-step verification.

11Children

Tessera is not directed at children under 13.
